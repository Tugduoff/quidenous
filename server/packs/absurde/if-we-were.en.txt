# title: If We Were…

# lang: en

If we were a rock band, who'd be the drummer who smashes everything?
If we were a rock band, who'd leave for a disastrous solo career?
If we were a kingdom, who'd be the monarch who does absolutely nothing?
If we were a kingdom, who'd be plotting in the castle corridors?
If we were a soup, who'd be the mystery chunk nobody expected?
If we were a sitcom, who'd be the neighbour who barges in without knocking?
If we were a sitcom, who'd get the laugh track on every single line?
If we planned a heist, who'd do the recon in a full ninja outfit in broad daylight?
If we planned a heist, who'd be the one who "knows a guy"?
If we were a pizza, who'd be the pineapple?
If we were a superhero team, who'd have the most useless power?
If we were a family of ducks, who'd waddle at the back and get lost?
If we were a bobsleigh team, who'd sit at the back yelling "faster"?
If we were a football team, who'd be the sub who celebrates goals harder than anyone?
If we were a circus, who'd volunteer to be the human cannonball?
If we were a musical, who'd burst into song at the worst possible moment?
If we were a summer camp, who'd be the counsellor who lost the kids?
If we were a cheeseboard, who'd be the stinky one everyone secretly loves?
If we were a pop group, who'd always be one beat behind on the choreography?
If we were a spaceship crew, who'd press the big red button "just to see"?
