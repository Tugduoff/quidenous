Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 (terms version 1). This page explains what data the qui de nous ? app uses, why, for how long, and how to delete it.

Controller

The publisher of qui de nous ? (coflop), reachable at contact@coflop.fr for any question about your data.

What we store

  • Your profile: first name, colour, drawn avatar or photo if you add one (cropped, with metadata such as location removed), friend code.
  • Your account, if you create one: e-mail address, password (stored only as an irreversible hash), two-factor settings, language, when you accepted the terms.
  • Sign in with Google or Discord, if you use it: your account ID with that service, your verified e-mail address and your name. We never receive your password and access nothing else.
  • The game: rooms, questions you add, votes, rankings, messages, reactions, published packs, question suggestions, friends.
  • Moderation: the reports you send (reason, details, a copy of the reported content) and the people you block.
  • Technical: sign-in sessions (device type, last used), push notification tokens, server logs, error reports when an app screen crashes (technical message, screen, version, platform, account ID). The app does not store your IP address: it is only used, in memory, to limit abuse.

No ads, no selling, no profiling, no analytics or tracking tools.

Why, and on what basis

  • Running the app (account, rooms, questions, results, messages, account e-mails such as the verification code or a forgotten password): performance of the contract, i.e. these terms.
  • Security and moderation (abuse limits, sessions, reports, blocks): our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe for everyone.
  • Push notifications: your consent, given in the app or the browser and withdrawable at any time in the settings.
  • Answering the authorities when the law requires it: legal obligation.

Who sees what

  • Members of your rooms see your profile and what you do in the room (votes, depending on the room's rules, messages).
  • Share links: when someone shares a question, a result or a stat, anyone with the link sees a card with the room's name, the question and, for a result or a stat, the first names and drawn avatars of the people in it (never a photo, never the votes). These links expire after 90 days, are kept out of search engines, and whoever made them (or the room's owner) can delete them in Profile > My links.
  • Public packs and creator pages: a pack you publish is visible to everyone, with your name; your creator page (name, photo or avatar, packs) is visible to signed-in users. 16+ packs are only shown to people who confirmed their age.
  • Question suggestions are shown to other players with your first name.
  • Challenges between rooms: the other room sees your room's name, and your podium (first names, drawn avatars) only if the shared link showed the result.

Service providers

The data is stored on a server we run ourselves. We use these providers, only for the service:

  • Mailgun (Sinch): sends the account e-mails. Depending on the configuration, its servers are in the European Union or in the United States; in the latter case the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Google: Sign in with Google if you use it, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications on Android.
  • Discord: Sign in with Discord if you use it, and a private team channel that receives the app's error reports.
  • Apple, Google, Mozilla: your phone's or browser's notification services, if you turn notifications on.
  • GitHub (Microsoft): only in the Android version installed outside the Play Store, to check for and download updates (GitHub then sees your IP address).

How long we keep it

  • Account and game content: as long as your account exists. When you delete it, your personal data is erased right away (profile, e-mail, photo, messages, sessions, notifications, friends, the share links you made and the stat cards you appear on); room history keeps your votes, anonymised.
  • Party guest accounts (no e-mail): erased automatically 30 days after their last party ends.
  • Sign-in sessions: until you sign out, revoke them (Profile > Security) or delete the account; a session unused for 180 days ends on its own.
  • Codes sent by e-mail: valid for a few minutes up to 30 minutes, erased a day after they expire.
  • Share links: 90 days (links made before November 2026 stay valid until deleted).
  • Reports: kept to handle the report and spot repeated abuse; if the reported person deletes their account, their name is removed from them.
  • Deleted rooms: no longer accessible to anyone; their content stays in the database until the accounts involved are deleted, or on request.
  • Backups: copies of the database kept for 30 days at most, then overwritten.
  • Server logs: size-capped (30 MB in all), the oldest dropped as new ones come in.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, restrict its use and object to it. To delete your account: in the app (Profile > Security > Delete my account) or on the Delete my account page. For anything else (including a copy of your data), write to us at contact@coflop.fr; we answer within a month. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Age

The app is for people aged 16 and over: everyone confirms it when accepting the terms. 16+ packs ask for one more confirmation.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, the app asks you to read and accept the new terms.
