Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026. This page explains what data the qui de nous ? app uses, why, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Your profile: first name, colour, photo if you add one, friend code.
  • Your account, if you create one: e-mail address, password (stored only in irreversibly encrypted form), two-factor authentication settings.
  • Sign-in with Google or Discord, if you use it: your account identifier with that service, your verified e-mail address and your name. We never receive your Google or Discord password and access nothing else.
  • The game: rooms, added questions, votes, rankings, messages and reactions.
  • Moderation: the reports you send (reason, reported content) and the people you block.
  • Technical: sign-in sessions (device, date of last use), push notification tokens, server technical logs kept for a short time.

Why

Only to make the app work: signing you in, showing questions and results to your room, sending notifications and account e-mails (verification code, forgotten password). No advertising, no reselling, no profiling.

Who has access

The members of your rooms see your profile and what you do in the room (votes according to the room's rules, messages). The data is stored on a server run by the qui de nous ? team. Providers used solely for the service: Mailgun (sending account e-mails), Google and Discord (if you sign in with them), the notification services of Apple, Google and web browsers.

How long

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account (how to) or ask us to, your personal data is erased right away; the rooms' history keeps your votes, anonymised.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to its processing. To delete your account: in the app (Profile > Security > Delete my account) or on the Delete my account page. For anything else, write to us: contact@coflop.fr. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

Age

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over.
