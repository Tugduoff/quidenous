Delete my account

You can delete your qui de nous ? account at any time. It is immediate and permanent.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: first name, photo, e-mail address, password, two-factor authentication, friend code.
  • Your sign-ins: sessions on all your devices, linked Google or Discord accounts, push notifications.
  • Your messages in rooms, your friends and the people you blocked.
  • Your places in rooms: you leave them all. If you were alone in a room, it is deleted; otherwise the person who joined earliest becomes its owner.

What stays, anonymised

So that everyone else's history stays accurate, your votes, rankings and added questions stay in the rooms, anonymised, with no link to you. The question packs you published also stay, without your name.

How to

In the app: Profile > Security > Delete my account.

Here, if your account has an e-mail address: we send you a code, you enter it, and the account is deleted.

If an account uses this address, a 6-digit code has just been sent to it (it expires in 30 minutes).

Account without an e-mail address (created with just a first name): delete it from the app, or write to us at contact@coflop.fr giving your first name and the name of your room.
