The service

qui de nous ? is a game between friends: one question at a time, everyone votes for someone in the room. The app is free and provided as is, with no guarantee of availability. It is published by coflop (contact@coflop.fr).

Age

The app is for people aged 16 and over. By accepting these terms, you confirm you are 16 or older. 16+ packs (daring questions) ask for one more confirmation.

Your account

You are responsible for your account and what you post. Keep your password to yourself; turn on two-factor authentication for more security. A party guest account (no e-mail) is erased 30 days after its last party ends.

Respect

Not allowed in questions, messages, names, photos, packs and suggestions: harassment, threats, hateful or discriminatory content, sexually explicit content, any content involving minors in a sexual way, illegal content or content meant to harm someone, other people's personal information.

In the app, a long press on a message, a question or a member's menu lets you report it or block the person; a pack is reported from its page. Reports reach us directly; we may remove the content and suspend or delete an account that breaks these rules. Room owners can remove members, and a public pack reported several times is hidden until reviewed.

Your content

You keep ownership of the questions and messages you write. You allow us to show them to the members of your rooms (and to everyone for the packs you publish) to run the app.

Public links

Sharing a question, a result or a stat makes a public link: anyone who has it sees the card, with the room's name and, for a result or a stat, the first names of the people in it. Share kindly. Links expire after 90 days; you can delete yours (and a room's owner, those of their room) in Profile > My links.

Ending

You can stop and delete your account at any time. These terms may change; after an important change, the app asks you to accept them again.
